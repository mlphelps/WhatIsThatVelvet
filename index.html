Our story

Made with intention. Sold with love.

The maker

A passion project
worth sharing.

Some of the best things start by accident. What Is That, Velvet? came to life during COVID as a creative outlet. A few jars shared with friends turned into something nobody expected — including me. Every time someone tried it, the reaction was the same — "this feels like velvet." The name wrote itself. (If you know, you know. 👑) Now every batch is made with the same love and intention as that very first one.
